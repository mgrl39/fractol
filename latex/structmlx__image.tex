\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
An image with an individual buffer that can be rendered. Any value can be modified except the width/height and context.


\begin{DoxyParams}{Parameters}
{\em width} & The width of the image. \\
\hline
{\em height} & The height of the image. \\
\hline
{\em pixels} & The literal pixel data. \\
\hline
{\em instances} & An instance carrying the X, Y and Z location data. \\
\hline
{\em count} & The element count of the instances array. \\
\hline
{\em enabled} & If true the image is drawn onto the screen, else it\textquotesingle{}s not. \\
\hline
{\em context} & Abstracted Open\+GL data. \\
