\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
An image instance can be summarized as just a simple x, y \& z coordinate.

Coordinates start from the top left of the screen at 0,0 and increase towards the bottom right.

NOTE\+: To change the z value, use mlx\+\_\+set\+\_\+instance\+\_\+depth!

\hline
{\em y} & The y location. \\
\hline
{\em z} & The z depth, controls if the image is on the fore or background. \\
\hline
{\em enabled} & If true, the instance is drawn, else it\textquotesingle{}s not. \\
